BRIEF

Pair Even G2 glasses with a short code, then load a demo Telegram inbox. Nothing is sent until you approve a draft on the glasses.

After pairing, the glasses show three or four unread highlights. Tap to open, tap again to draft, tap once more to send. Swipe to move. Double-tap the home briefing to exit.

Live Telegram Bot API is optional and is configured on the server, not in this page.
